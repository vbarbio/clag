# Privacy Policy - CLAG (Clear Labels & Archive for Gmail)

Last updated: 19 November 2026

CLAG does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- The extension only runs on mail.google.com, app.jace.ai and mail.superhuman.com.
- On those pages it looks for the label chips of the thread you have open and clicks them away,
  then archives the thread, exactly as you would do by hand.
- It makes no network requests, uses no analytics, no cookies and no browser storage.
- It does not read the content of your emails for any purpose other than finding the label buttons on screen.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/vbarbio/clag/issues
